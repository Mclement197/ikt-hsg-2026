# P3 · Management reporting and planning

Explore how source records become validated analysis, an approved management report, and a documented capacity decision.

## Open and operate

Extract the ZIP completely, then double-click `index.html`. Keep its accompanying files together. No installation, server, account or internet connection is needed. Each ZIP is an independent mock case.

- Use the portal’s forms and action buttons. **You are acting as** identifies the responsible person or team.
- **Perform suggested action** fills the example values and presses the corresponding portal button once.
- **Play example** advances automatically; **Pause example** lets you discuss a decision.
- **Change scenario** starts another case. Invalid input blocks progression; business exceptions may enter correction or unresolved-outcome paths.

## Read the live EPC / EPK

Amber marks the current activity or event; teal marks completed work.

- **Follow** keeps the active position visible. **Current step** recentres and resumes following.
- **Overview** shows the whole diagram and turns following off. Zoom, drag or expand the map to inspect it.
- Select a shape for its meaning and activity details, including responsibilities and data.
- **Show roles, systems and data** reveals the supporting objects.

## Available scenarios

| Scenario | What it demonstrates |
|---|---|
| 4 April · Final assessments | Five assessed ownership segments for 2028/29. Run both analyses, approve and release the report, then save a capacity plan. |
| 3 January · Midyear forecast | Alice’s issued assessment and four matching estimates cover all five segments. |
| 3 January · Missing buyer case | Ben’s registered segment has no case. See which totals are withheld and route the exception to source correction. |
| Management requests a revision | Valid figures need a clearer coverage statement, so the revised report returns to review. |
| A defect discovered during review | New MOT evidence changes an assessment, requiring source correction and both analyses to run again. |

## Suggested 2–3 minute presentation

1. Open **3 January · Midyear forecast**. The April–March billing year is **2028/29** for both Alice and Ben. Their shared vehicle counts once. BEV: 2 vehicles, 21,000 miles, £630. PHEV: 2 vehicles, 18,000 miles, £270. Expected final tax: **£900**, zero incomplete segments.
2. Switch to **3 January · Missing buyer case** and validate. BEV still has two vehicles, but one segment is incomplete: its miles and tax and the grand total are withheld. PHEV still shows £270. Send the correction request. Open Ben’s case with the verified 5,000-mile estimate on 4 January, then extract at the **5 January** cutoff, revalidate and rerun both analyses. The repair does not change what was available on 3 January.
3. Open **4 April · Final assessments**. Run just one analysis to see compilation wait for the second. Each type has 2 vehicles and 22,000 assessed miles: £660 BEV + £330 PHEV = **£990**. The five original advance demands total **£1,020**; comparing each with its issued tax gives £90 gross additions and £120 gross refunds. The database’s separate cash reconciliation gives **£960 net received** and **£30 outstanding**.
4. Approve, release and plan for three additional-tax cases and one refund. To compare review paths, try the wording revision or the source defect: correcting Ben’s closing reading from 31,500 to 32,000 miles requires a new snapshot and changes gross additions from £75 to £90.

A wording revision reuses the validated outputs. A source correction requires a new snapshot, validation and both analyses. The forecast uses the issued assessment available at the cutoff, or a current accepted estimate only while the segment is open and its coverage matches exactly. A valid zero is complete; missing evidence is not zero.

## Replay and export

**Undo action** restores the preceding state. **Restart scenario**, changing scenario or reloading starts a fresh run. State is held in memory and is not restored after closing the browser.

**Activity log** explains each action. **Download run log** exports the mock data, history and outcome as JSON for inspection; it does not provide a saved-game import.

## Scope

All people, vehicles, records, evidence and transactions are fictional. No real authentication, submissions, payments or external connections occur. The case assumes BEV 3p/mile and PHEV 1.5p/mile, with calculated money rounded to the nearest penny.

EPC means *Event-driven Process Chain*; EPK is *Ereignisgesteuerte Prozesskette*. Events describe states, functions describe work, XOR selects one alternative, and AND requires all parallel branches.
